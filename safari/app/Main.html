Clink

Clean links. Never broken ones.

  • Removes tracking tags like utm_source, fbclid and gclid from the links you open.
  • Skips click-tracking redirects, so you go straight to the page.
  • Keeps everything a link needs to work. Nothing leaves your device.

Turn on Clink

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions (on iOS 17: Settings → Safari → Extensions).
  2. Tap Clink and turn it on.
  3. Set All Websites to Allow, so Clink can clean links on every site.

On Clink is on in Safari.

Off Clink is off in Safari.

In Safari’s Settings → Extensions, turn on Clink, then choose Always Allow on Every Website.
